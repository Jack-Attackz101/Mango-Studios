04 / Mission

Help defense contractors build their own software.

We are building ALLMIGHT for the small and midsize companies that make parts, manage programs and solve engineering problems across the Defense Industrial Base.

i.

Why we are building it

The people who understand the work should be able to shape the software around it, without starting every application from scratch.

ii.

What it is designed to do

Turn a described workflow into a plan, an application and a controlled path through testing, security review and release.

iii.

Who stays in control

The planned workflow keeps plan approval, permissions and release decisions with the customer. Optional engineering support is also planned.
