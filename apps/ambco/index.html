    Who we are

    A century of precision, made in America.

    American Metal Bearing Company began in 1921, when Mr. Wyllis opened a small bearing shop in Los Angeles to make specialty bearings for early Indy cars.

    Through the twentieth century the company pioneered advances in journal bearing design. Since the Second World War it has engineered and supplied critical propulsion components for the marine and naval industries, and it has been the primary supplier of bearing propulsion systems to the U.S. Navy for more than 85 years.

    In 1973 AMB moved to its current home in Garden Grove, California. With over 100 years of experience, the company is still dedicated to superior quality and precision in every product it makes.
