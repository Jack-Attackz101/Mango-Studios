Heritage · 1921 → today

A century of turning shafts.

In 1921 a small shop in Los Angeles began machining specialty bearings for the earliest Indy cars. Through the twentieth century it pioneered advances in journal bearing design — and the list of Navy ships and submarines running on AMB bearings reads like the history of the U.S. fleet.

Stave & partial arc bearings

Lubricated by the sea itself.

AMB's water-lubricated bearings are manufactured in stave and partial arc systems for conventional and nuclear-powered submarines, naval surface vessels and industrial equipment — using the ocean as the lubricant, with nothing to leak into it.

Where power meets water.
