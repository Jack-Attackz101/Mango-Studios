What we do

Hydrodynamic bearings, from first model to final part.

AMB specializes in hydrodynamic bearing design for marine, industrial and specialized applications. In-house design and engineering teams carry each project from concept to final product.

Advanced manufacturing

From one-of-a-kind designs to large-scale continuous production. High-tolerance, precision components with complex specifications, made on modern multi-axis CNC equipment. Babbitt has been cast in house since 1921.

    Our story

    American Metal Bearing Company began in 1921, when Mr. Wyllis opened a small bearing shop in Los Angeles to make specialty bearings for early Indy cars.

    Through the twentieth century the company pioneered advances in journal bearing design. Since the Second World War it has engineered and supplied critical propulsion components for the marine and naval industries, playing a vital role in national security.

    For more than 85 years AMB has been the primary supplier of bearing propulsion systems to the U.S. Navy. In 1973 the company moved to its current home in Garden Grove, California, and with over 100 years of experience it remains dedicated to superior quality and precision in every product it makes.
