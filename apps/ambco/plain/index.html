P-01 · Oil lubricated

Line shaft bearings

AMB began engineering and producing oil-lubricated bearings in 1921 and is the premier engineering manufacturer of line shaft bearings to the U.S. Navy and its allies throughout the world.

  • Found on almost every large U.S. Navy surface combatant
  • Babbitted bearings centrifugally cast in house
  • Tested to NAVSEA and ABS approved procedures with in-house NDT

P-02 · Water lubricated

Propulsor bearings

Water-lubricated bearings manufactured in stave and partial arc systems for conventional and nuclear-powered submarines, naval surface vessels and industrial equipment.

  • Dual-hardness, low-friction nitrile rubber staves approved by the U.S. Navy
  • Grooves between the staves carry seawater through, lubricating the bearing and flushing out sand and abrasives
  • Partial arc (round bore) bearings held to exacting tolerances
Full specifications ↗

P-03 · Tilting pad

Thrust bearings

AMB can produce a tilting pad thrust bearing whether it is small or over 30,000 pounds, designed and built for propulsion shafting and industrial equipment.

  • No lube oil pumping system or jacking pumps required
  • Self-lubricating in every operating condition, including jacking speeds
  • Self-aligning, load-leveling design allows shaft deflection while minimizing wear
Full specifications ↗

P-04 · Sealing systems

Keeping the ocean where it belongs

Where a rotating shaft passes through a pressure hull there is no margin for error. AMB sealing systems are engineered alongside the bearings so the whole stern assembly works as one.

  • Designed together with the bearing system
  • Built for full-depth service on submarines

Our legacy

In 1921, a small Los Angeles shop began making bearings for Indy cars.

Mr. Wyllis founded American Metal Bearing at 3rd Street and Central Avenue. Casting babbitt was a core skill from day one, and the company pioneered advances in journal bearing design through the twentieth century.
