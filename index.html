About us

Mango Studios is a small software studio. We build websites, apps, AI agents and automations for businesses, and we make our own apps too. When you work with us, you work with the person building your project, from the first email to launch day. You get a straight answer on what it costs, then we get to work.

FAQ

How much does a project cost?

Custom sites and apps start at $250, AI agents at $500 and automations at $300. AI Consulting is a flat $100 per session. After one conversation you get a real quote for your project.

Who will I be working with?

The person building your project. There are no account managers, so you talk directly to Jack, James or Hayden.

How fast can you start?

We keep scoping short. Once we agree on the quote, work starts.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. We work with single shops and full operations alike.

Do you use AI to build?

Yes. AI agents help us write, test and research, and we review everything they make before it ships.

What happens in an AI Consulting session?

A plain conversation about how your business runs, where AI would help and what isn't worth doing yet. You can book one on the AI Consulting page.

Where are you based?

Toronto, Ontario.
