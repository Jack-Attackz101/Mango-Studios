About us

Mango Studios is a small software studio. We build websites, apps, AI agents and automations for businesses, and we make our own apps too.

When you work with us, you work with the person building your project, from the first email to launch day. You get a straight answer on what it costs, then we get to work.

  • Straight to the builder

    The person you email is the person doing the work, so nothing gets lost in a hand-off.

  • Prices up front

    One conversation gets you a real quote and an honest answer on whether we're a fit.

  • We ship

    Tight scope, quick turnaround and a working product at the end.
