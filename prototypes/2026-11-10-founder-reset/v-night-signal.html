The science behind the ingredients

Independent research on the key ingredients. These studies looked at the ingredients, not the finished Founder Reset product.

  • Creatine

    Supports strength and power during high-intensity exercise, with consistent daily use.*

    The International Society of Sports Nutrition reviewed research on creatine in exercise and training. At 3 g a day without a loading phase, muscle creatine stores typically build up over around four weeks.

    • International Society of Sports Nutrition position stand (2017)

  • Vitamin B12

    Needed for normal energy metabolism, red blood cell formation and nervous system function.*

    The NIH fact sheet covers B12 intake and absorption. Supplementing matters most when intake or absorption is low, which is more common with age and plant-based diets. Additional B12 has not been shown to improve energy or endurance when B12 status is adequate.

    • NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, Vitamin B12 fact sheet

  • Magnesium

    Supports normal muscle and nerve function and is involved in more than 300 enzyme systems, including energy metabolism.*

    The NIH fact sheet notes that many US adults get less magnesium from food than recommended. It reviews nutritional roles and intake, rather than testing the finished Founder Reset product.

    • NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, Magnesium fact sheet

  • Glycine

    An amino acid studied for its role in sleep quality.*

    Small studies in Japan gave volunteers 3 g of glycine before bed. Yamadera and colleagues (2007) measured self-reported sleep quality and sleep recordings in people with unsatisfactory sleep. Bannai and colleagues (2012) studied next-day fatigue and performance during partial sleep restriction. These studies were small and involved industry-affiliated researchers.

    • Bannai et al., Frontiers in Neurology (2012)
    • Yamadera et al., Sleep and Biological Rhythms (2007)

  • L-theanine

    An amino acid found in tea, studied for relaxation.*

    Hidese and colleagues (2019) gave 30 healthy adults 200 mg a day of L-theanine or placebo for four weeks in a randomized crossover trial, measuring stress-related symptoms, sleep and cognitive function. The study was small and received industry support.

    • Hidese et al., Nutrients (2019)

* These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
